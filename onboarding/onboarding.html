🔒 Privacy & Terms

Before we start, please read how your data is handled:

  • Your resume (PDF/DOCX) is read in your browser and only its keywords are stored locally — never the full file.
  • Job descriptions are scraped from LinkedIn and processed in-memory. They are never stored.
  • Your data is sent to Google's Gemini AI API using your own API key for keyword extraction. By continuing, you consent to this processing.
  • The developer (Jayadev) operates no servers and has no access to your data.
  • You can delete all stored data at any time from the extension settings.

This extension is provided "as is". Jayadev accepts no liability for outcomes related to job applications or data processed through the Gemini API.
